Frequently Asked Questions

How does the image piece recommendation work?

When you upload a garment photo, our backend loads a deep 50-layer Convolutional Neural Network (ResNet50). It removes the classification layer and extracts a 2048-dimensional mathematical feature representation of the clothing's silhouette, textures, and contours. It then runs nearest-neighbor vector search to surface the top 6 closest matching items in our database.

What models power the outfit chat assistant?

Our assistant operates on a multi-tier AI engine. It primarily uses Google Gemini 1.5 Flash, with automatic failover to Meta LLaMA 3 70B on Groq for ultra-fast styling responses, and an offline expert rules engine if network connectivity is interrupted.

Is FitFusion free to use?

Yes! Free registration grants you a generous monthly query allowance (100 queries/month) across image similarity matching and conversational outfit suggestions.

Are my uploaded photos kept private?

Absolutely. Uploaded images are strictly used for your active session to extract visual features and compute similarity rankings. They are never shared or sold to third parties.
